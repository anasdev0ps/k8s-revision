1 Analogy (Samajhne ke liye)

  • Readiness: "Are you ready to take calls?" — Yeh ek Traffic Gate hai. Jab tak ready nahi ho, traffic nahi aayega.
  • Liveness: "Are you still alive or stuck?" — Yeh Restart Trigger hai. Agar app stuck hai, toh container ko maar kar naya chala dega.
  • Startup: "Give slow starters extra time before we judge them." — Slow boot hone wali apps ko time deta hai taake Liveness unhein jaldi na maar de.

2 Core Concept

  • Readiness Service traffic ko control karta hai (agar fail ho toh Endpoint se pod remove ho jata hai).
  • Liveness hung ya freeze container ko restart karta hai.
  • Startup slow-boot apps ko premature liveness kills se protect karta hai.
  • Probe Types (Checks): httpGet (API check), tcpSocket (port check), exec (script run karna), grpc.

3 Hands-on Steps

  • Apne Pod mein teeno (3) probes add karein.
  • Readiness fail karein: Dekhein kaise Pod Service ke Endpoints se drop (gayab) ho jata hai.
  • Liveness fail karein: Dekhein kaise Pod ke restarts badhna (climb) shuru ho jate hain.

4 Errors to Break + Fix

  • Error 1: Wrong probe path ya port dena. Isse Liveness fail hogi aur pod CrashLoopBackOff state mein chala jayega.
  • Error 2: Too-tight initialDelaySeconds rakhna (app on hone se pehle hi fail kar dena).

5 Best Practices (Pro Tips)

  • Readiness hamesha real dependency check par lagayen (e.g., DB ready hai ya nahi).
  • Liveness hamesha lightweight honi chahiye (heavy query na lagayen).
  • Slow apps ke liye HAMESHA startupProbe set karein.
  • Liveness ko kabhi bhi external services par depend na karein warna bahar ka issue aapke pods ko restart kar dega.

✅ Done-Check

  • Traffic ek not-ready pod ki taraf aana stop ho gaya hai, **lekin pod kill (restart) nahi hua**. (Yeh Readiness ka proof hai!)
