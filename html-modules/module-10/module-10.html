1 Core Concepts

  • Deployment: Stateless apps ke liye (jahan pod ka naam badal bhi jaye toh faraq nahi parta).
  • StatefulSet: Stable identity aur storage ke liye (jaise Databases). In pods ke naam hamesha fix hotay hain jaise pod-0, pod-1. Agar pod-0 maray ga toh naya anay wala pod bhi pod-0 hi kehlayega!
  • DaemonSet: Iska rule simple hai: Cluster mein mojood HAA node par iska 1 pod lazmi chalega (e.g. logging agent ya monitoring tool). Agar naya node add ho, toh ek pod us par auto-create ho jayega.
  • Job: Yeh ek task ko (run-to-completion) karta hai. Iska maqsad app ko forever chalana nahi hai, balke kaam khatam kar ke terminate (Die) ho jana hai.
  • CronJob: Ek Job jo Linux Cron ki tarah ek muqarrar waqt (scheduled time) par automatically chalti hai.

2 Hands-on Steps

  1. Ek StatefulSet create karein jisme per-pod PVC attach ho. Observe karein ke pods strictly order mein create hotay hain (0, phir 1, phir 2).
  2. Ek DaemonSet banayen aur check karein ke wo sab nodes par 1-1 deploy ho gaya hai.
  3. Ek Job YAML likhein jo complete hone par ruk jaye.
  4. Ek CronJob banayen jo har minute (* * * * *) chale aur log mein hello print kare.

3 Errors to Break + Fix

  • StatefulSet stuck on PVC: Agar StorageClass available nahi hai, toh pod-0 Pending rahega, aur pod-1 tab tak nahi banega jab tak 0 complete na ho! (Head-of-line blocking).
  • Job backoff on failure: Agar Job ka andar wala script fail hota rahey, toh Kubernetes usay bar bar chalata hai (Backoff Limit tak) aur phir rok deta hai.

4 Best Practices (Pro Tips)

  • StatefulSets sirf ordered ya identity-dependent workloads (Databases, Kafka, Zookeeper) ke liye hi use karein.
  • DaemonSet pods mein hamesha Tolerations add karein taake wo Tainted nodes (jaise master nodes ya GPU nodes) par bhi ja kar log collect kar sakein.
  • Job YAML mein hamesha backoffLimit aur ttlSecondsAfterFinished lagayen, warna purani failed jobs cluster mein kachra (garbage) jama karti rahengi!

Done-Check

  • StatefulSet sequentially chala, DaemonSet har node par phel gaya, Job apna kaam pura kar ke ruk gayi, aur CronJob schedule par chali. All workloads behaving as expected!
